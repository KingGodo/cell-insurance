RISK

How close they are to leaving.

Risk is the chance a client leaves while they are still on the book. It is scored from the way they claim and the way they pay, so the intervention can happen before the exit.

  • Claim frequency
  • Sentiment around claims
  • Renewal premium shock
  • Seasonal pressure in the rainy and school-fee months
  • Premiums paid from outside the country
  • Days late
  • Uneven gaps between the due date and the payment
  • Cash or a diaspora transfer as the usual channel
