SOLUTION

Profile the client, then act while they are still here.

CustomerIQ scores every client on value and on risk, and sends a retention plan the moment those figures match a rule that was set first.

  • Build a profile that shows what the client is worth and how close they are to leaving.
  • Intervene before a lapse or a cancellation, while keeping them is still cheaper than replacing them.
  • Hold each plan until a rule sets the value and risk a client must meet.
  • Put a cost on the plan, and send it by SMS, email, socials, marketing, customer service, or the portal.
